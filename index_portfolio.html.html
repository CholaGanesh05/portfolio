How I try to work

The Bhagavad Gita shapes how I approach my craft and my days. These four ideas are the ones I come back to.

Karmaṇy evādhikāras teBhagavad Gita 2.47

Your right is to the action, not to its fruits. I give my full attention to building each thing well, and let results follow.

Yogaḥ karmasu kauśalamBhagavad Gita 2.50

Skill in action is a discipline in itself. That means careful design, clean code, and systems I’ve tested before I ask anyone to trust them.

Samatvaṁ yoga ucyateBhagavad Gita 2.48

Stay even-minded in success and in failure. I debug calmly and treat a failed build or a rejected idea as information.

LokasaṅgrahaBhagavad Gita 3.20

Act for the good of the world. I want my software to be useful to real people, which is also why I run AI literacy workshops and join clean-up drives.

First Prize, EpochOn 2.0 HackathonNyaya Sarathi: an AI legal assistant that finds the relevant sections of the Indian Constitution for a problem.2026

Nyaya Sarathi is a legal assistance agent built with LangChain, LangGraph and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). It draws on records of the Indian Constitution, and for a given problem it finds the most relevant sections and returns them as a clear answer. The project won first prize at EpochOn 2.0.

First Prize, Smart India Hackathon (internal round)Krishi Sahay: an all-in-one assistant for farmers, with a voice agent, disease detection and tool rental.2025

Krishi Sahay brings the things a farmer needs into one place. It has a voice agent using Gemini and an IVR system integrated with AI, so farmers can get help by speaking. It can identify a crop disease from a photo, and it offers carpooling, market prices and weather details such as temperature. Farmers can also rent tools they need from other farmers. It won first prize in the internal round of the Smart India Hackathon.

First Prize, Debate CompetitionArgued for love marriage in a debate on love versus arranged marriage.2024

The topic was love marriage versus arranged marriage, and our side argued for love marriage. Our main point was that, in today’s world, people should get to know each other before committing to a marriage. We accepted that arranged marriage suited older generations, because their times were different from ours. We also raised the position of the LGBTQ community, where arranged marriage leaves open questions. We won first prize.

Top 10, ICSRF National HackathonA solar system simulation showing distances between satellites and how they perform.2025

At the ICSRF national hackathon, my team built a simulation of the solar system, organised planet by planet. It lets you find the distances between satellites and see how satellites could perform within the solar system. We finished in the top 10.

Third Prize, GreenCode Challenge at VidyutA solar panel layout planner for a piece of land.2025

For the GreenCode Challenge, we built a system that takes a piece of land and helps plan how to arrange solar panels on it, along with the solar energy involved. We won third prize.

AI literacy workshop, St. George Public School“The Dangers of AI”: a session for Class 9 and 11 students on deepfakes, misinformation and over-reliance on AI chatbots.

As part of Amrita’s Student Social Responsibility (SSR) initiative, our team of six ran a session called “The Dangers of AI” at St. George Public School in Chengannur, Kerala, for students of Class 9 and Class 11. AI tools are now part of students’ daily lives, so we wanted to turn their curiosity into awareness of the risks: deepfakes, misinformation and emotional dependence on AI. The session included a deepfake-spotting challenge, a “Boys vs. Girls” AI quiz, real-life misinformation case studies, and a discussion on relying emotionally on AI chatbots. The projector wasn’t available on the day, so we adapted: we delivered the session verbally, used laptops for demos, and kept the room engaged. It taught me that adaptability matters more than perfection, and that teaching is one of the best ways to learn. We were mentored by Gowry S Kumar of Amrita’s CIR Department.
